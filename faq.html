Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Julius Care and our non-medical care services.

Common Questions

What type of care does Julius Care provide?
Julius Care provides non-medical home care support including companionship, personal assistance, household support, and daily living assistance.
Who can receive care from Julius Care?
We support seniors, individuals needing assistance, and families looking for reliable non-medical care support.
Does Julius Care provide medical services?
No. Julius Care provides non-medical care services and does not replace medical professionals or healthcare providers.
Can care plans be personalized?
Yes. Care support can be adjusted based on each client's needs, preferences, and daily routines.
How do I request care services?
You can contact Julius Care through our Request Care page, email, or phone number to discuss your needs.
Where does Julius Care provide services?
Julius Care provides non-medical care services in Oye Ekiti, Nigeria and surrounding areas.
How can family members stay involved?
We encourage communication with families to ensure clients receive comfortable, respectful, and dependable support.
